## Crop table

Seasonal water needs are the midpoint of the range in [FAO Irrigation Water Management, Training manual no. 3, Table 5](https://www.fao.org/4/s2022e/s2022e02.htm). Chickpea and lentil use that table's bean range, faba bean uses the pea range, and sesame uses the oil-seed range in [FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 24](https://www.fao.org/4/s8376e/s8376e.pdf). Salt classes follow FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 29.

##Summary
FARM CASE 🌱🔎
Every Farm Has a Mystery… Can You Solve It?
What if your farm became a mystery, where every problem holds a clue?
In FARM CASE, you become a farm detective, exploring your farm to discover better ways to protect it and improve its future.
Start by entering your farm details, such as its location, soil type, water source, and previous crops. Then explore an interactive map to collect clues about climate, water, vegetation, and soil.
Using AI and NASA Earth observation data, FARM CASE connects these clues, analyzes farm conditions, and compares crop rotation strategies.
At the end, you receive a suggested crop rotation plan, alternative options, and insights into water needs and potential risks.
FARM CASE turns complex Earth data into an interactive investigation that supports smarter decisions and more sustainable agriculture.

Collect the clues, solve the case, and create a better future for your farm!
